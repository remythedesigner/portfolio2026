The brief

A working product that didn't yet look trustworthy

Yogspace connects students with certified yoga teachers for live, 1:1 classes taught in or inspired by traditional Indian lineages. Unlike Terroo, this wasn't a blank page: the founder had already built and shipped it.

What Yogspace was selling was credibility: a verified certificate, a real lineage, a teacher worth paying for over a free YouTube video. The site didn't say any of that visually. Rounded pill shapes, a chunky display font, flat cream blocks with no depth, it read closer to a kids' app than a platform asking people to trust a stranger with their practice. I wasn't inventing a brand from nothing here. I was closing the gap between what the product actually did and what it looked like it did.

Art direction

Four decisions behind the redesign

01 An editorial serif, not a rounded mascot font

The original type was a chunky, rounded display face that made a certification platform read as playful. I moved headlines to EB Garamond, paired with Karla for UI text, closer to a hospitality brand than a fitness app.

Learn yoga from its source.

EB Garamond / Karla

02 Away from the wellness-teal cliché

Every yoga app reaches for the same sage green or spa teal. I kept a warm terracotta as the single accent against cream and a near-black navy for UI weight, so it reads closer to a considered travel or hospitality brand than a template.

03 Trust made visible, not just stated

"Certified" meant nothing as a plain word in a paragraph. I designed a small set of custom badge icons (top rated, certified, specialist) and turned each teacher's certification history into an actual timeline, so the credential is something you see, not something you take on faith.

⭐ 🏅 🕐
04 Fewer pills, more air

The original leaned on pill shapes for nearly every container, with almost no shadow or depth to separate them. Reintroducing soft, warm-toned shadows and letting some sections go full ink gives the page rhythm instead of one continuous cream block.

The result: a real component library

Navigation, buttons, form fields, auth cards, icons, all generated and organized in the same file the founder works in every day.
